# Interview Guide — Social Music · v1
2026-11-03 · target: 18–29 in the US whose music taste is part of their identity (see icp-hypothesis-2026-11-03.md) · assumptions under test: #1–#5

> Written in English because the target customers are in the US (plugin rule: interview language = customers' language). Explicação em português para o fundador no fim. 15–20 minutes. Never mention a badge or a product.

## Intro script (verbatim)
"Hi, I'm Arthur — I'm researching how people your age show the music they're into, online and offline. I'm not selling anything; there may never be a product. I just want to learn how it works for you today. Is it OK if I take notes?"

## Warm-up (context, 2 min)
1. Tell me a bit about how music fits into your week — when and where do you listen? *(→ public listening; filters "background only" people)*

## Core — past behavior (10 min)
2. What's an album or artist you got really into recently? How did other people around you find out — or did they? *(→ the core pain; #1)*
   ↳ probe: When was the last time someone noticed what you listen to without you telling them? What happened?
3. Think about the last thing you bought that was about music but not for listening — a shirt, pin, poster, vinyl you don't play. Walk me through it: what, where, how much, why? *(→ #1, #5: real spend)*
   ↳ probe: Do you still wear or show it? How often?
4. How has what you listen to changed over the last few months? Do the things you wear still match it? *(→ #4: does taste outrun static merch)*
   ↳ probe: Have you ever stopped wearing a band shirt because you weren't into that music anymore?
5. Where do you share what you listen to today — Wrapped, stories, Last.fm, Airbuds, telling friends? When did you last do it, and what happened after? *(→ #1: free digital substitute)*
6. Has there been a time you didn't want people to see or hear what you were listening to? What was going on? *(→ #3)*
7. What devices do you carry every day that need charging? When did one last die on you, and what did you do? *(→ #2)*
   ↳ probe: Which ones do you just stop using when charging gets annoying?
8. What's the most annoying or most fun part of being someone who's really into music, day to day? *(open door — often reveals the real problem)*

## Ranking (2 min)
9. Of what we talked about — being noticed for your taste, showing it through stuff you wear, sharing it online — which one matters most to you, and which could you live without?

## Closing — commitment ladder (3 min)
10. Can I follow up in a few weeks when I know more? → get a date or contact
11. Who else do you know who's like this about music? Could you introduce me to 1–2 of them?
12. (only if a prototype exists later) Would you try something early and give me honest feedback?

## DO NOT
- pitch the idea, mention a badge, screens or features, or ask "would you use/pay/like".
- accept compliments as answers — redirect to "tell me about the last time…".

## Para o fundador (pt)
- **Duração e meta:** 15–20 min por conversa; 10–15 entrevistas antes de sintetizar. Anote as frases exatas.
- **Regra principal:** não fale da ideia. Pergunte sobre o passado deles, não sobre o futuro. Elogio não conta como dado.
- **Mais valiosas:** as perguntas 3 e 4 (o que já gastaram e se o gosto muda mais rápido que a camiseta).
- **Depois de 10 conversas:** coloque uma nota por pessoa em `01-discovery/interviews/` e rode a síntese (customers Mode B).
